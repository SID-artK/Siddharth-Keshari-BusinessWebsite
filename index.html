About the business

What we do

We collect parcels from homes, shops and offices and deliver them within Vadodara the same day. Every order gets an Order ID that customers can use to track it.

Who we serve

Small online sellers, local shops, offices sending documents, and families sending gifts or essentials across the city.

Service area

All of Vadodara city: Alkapuri, Akota, Gotri, Manjalpur, Makarpura, Fatehgunj, Karelibaug, and nearby areas up to the ring road.

Why customers choose us

Same-day delivery with live tracking, at a fixed price that is known before booking.
