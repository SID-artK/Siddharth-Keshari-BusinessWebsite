# Sayaji Express Logistics: Business Website

**Student:** Siddharth Keshri | **Course:** Information Technology for Business Transformation (ITBT), MBA LSCM, Gati Shakti Vishwavidyalaya

## Business
Sayaji Express Logistics is a local logistics and delivery service in Vadodara.

## Problem solved
Customers do not know where their parcel is, and managers cannot see delays quickly.

## What the website has
- Home, About, Services (with prices), Dashboard (8 KPIs and 2 charts), Track Order, Contact and Enquiry
- Business feature: **Order tracking** (try SEL1025, SEL1031, SEL1042, SEL1017)
- Working hyperlinks: internal navigation, Get Started, Book this, Dashboard to Track Order, Google Maps, email, phone, LinkedIn, PM Gati Shakti portal
